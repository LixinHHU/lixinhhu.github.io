Hi there, I am **Xin Li (李鑫)**. I am an Associate Professor in the [College of Computer and Software](https://cies.hhu.edu.cn/) at [Hohai University](https://www.hhu.edu.cn/), Nanjing, China. I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science and Technology from Hohai University in 2021.

My research interests include **remote sensing image processing**, **multimodal data fusion**, and **domain-adaptive semantic segmentation**. My work has appeared in journals including *Information Fusion*, *IEEE Transactions on Geoscience and Remote Sensing*, and *Knowledge-Based Systems*.

I was an Assistant Researcher / Postdoctoral Researcher at Hohai University from 2022 to 2023 and have been a Young Associate Professor since January 2024.
